Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

In short: Runell has no account, no servers, no analytics and no ads, and doesn't track you. Your training data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. The developer receives none of it.

Who is responsible

Runell is developed by Martin Gertjegerdes, who is responsible for the app's data processing. Contact: runell@gertjegerdes.de.

Data Runell stores

Your training plans, workouts, the answers you give when creating a plan, your running shoes and your settings. They are stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, synced through your private iCloud database, which only you can access. Text you enter, such as plan names, notes and shoe names, is end-to-end encrypted in iCloud. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Apple Health

With your permission, Runell reads workouts and their routes, heart rate, active and resting energy, running power, steps, VO2 max and running speed. It uses them to suggest your starting point, mark sessions as completed, show your runs, trends and best times, and adapt your plan. Health data is read on your device, and Runell doesn't store or sync your Health records or routes; routes are only used to draw a run's map and work out its splits. When you create a plan, a few summary values, such as your recent weekly running, can fill in your starting profile, which is saved encrypted with the plan. Runell never writes to Health, and Health data is never used for advertising or shared with third parties. Runell works without Health access, and you can withdraw access at any time in Settings › Health.

Apple services the app uses

  • Apple Watch: workouts are scheduled into Apple's Workout app with WorkoutKit.
  • Apple Maps: to name the place a run started, the run's starting coordinates are sent to Apple's geocoding service.
  • Apple Intelligence: Coach's Take is written by the on-device model, so its facts never leave your device. When you use Modify with AI, your request and a summary of the plan (its weeks, dates and workouts) are processed by Apple's Private Cloud Compute, which doesn't store them.
  • Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight and widgets: answers are worked out on your device. Plan names and overviews are added to your device's Spotlight index. Siri may speak a plan name or your next run aloud or show it on the Lock Screen.

Plan problem reports

If you choose to report a problem with a plan, Runell creates a file with the coach's input, app versions and the plan as shown. It contains no plan name, notes, calendar dates or Health records, only the summary values the coach used. It leaves your device only if you share it, and is then used only to investigate the problem.

What Runell doesn't do

  • No account or sign-in, and no developer servers.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and no data sold or shared with data brokers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Delete a plan from its menu, or use Delete All Training Data in Runell's Settings to remove every plan and running shoe from your device and iCloud. Deleting the app removes the data stored only on the device.

Your rights

Because the developer receives no personal data from the app, your data is under your control on your devices and in iCloud. If you email support, your message is used only to answer you and is deleted when no longer needed. Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete and restrict the processing of your data, to data portability, to object, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

Children

Runell isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is published on this page with a new effective date.
