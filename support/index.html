Frequently asked questions

Where do my workouts appear on Apple Watch?

In Apple's Workout app. Runell has no Watch app of its own; it schedules structured workouts with warm-up, intervals, cool-down and pace and heart-rate alerts. Your current plan syncs when you open Runell and after changes. You can also sync a plan or a single workout by hand, or use Sync to Watch from Control Center, the Lock Screen or the Action button.

A workout isn't on my Watch.

Make sure workout scheduling is allowed for Runell and your Watch is paired, then open the plan and sync it again. Only workouts from today onward are scheduled, by day and without a start time, and Apple limits how many can be scheduled at once.

Do I need Apple Health?

No. Runell works without Health: answer the questions in plan creation and it builds your plan from them. With Health access, Runell can fill in your starting point, mark sessions as completed and show your runs, trends and best times. Runell only reads from Health and never writes to it. You can change access in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Runell.

Why wasn't a run counted?

A recorded run counts for a planned session within a day of it, one run per session. Check that the workout was recorded as a run and is in Health. Earlier sessions without a matching run are marked as missed; you can reschedule them or move a missed run to a free next day.

How does sync between my devices work?

Plans and running shoes sync through your private iCloud database. Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive turned on. Settings in Runell shows the iCloud sync status.

What needs Apple Intelligence?

Modify with AI, which turns a request in your own words into a plan change, and the dashboard's Coach's Take need a device with Apple Intelligence turned on and a supported language. Everything else, including plan creation and Rebuild From My Recorded Running, works without it.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a single plan from its menu, or use Delete All Training Data in Settings to remove every plan and running shoe from this device and iCloud. Deleting the app removes data stored only on the device.
